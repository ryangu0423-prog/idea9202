Videos are compressed 720p web copies, approximately 6 seconds each. Keep original video artwork. Treat clips 1–6 as state loops and clips 7–10 as one-way transitions. In Group mode, sustained severe noise progresses through the highly agitated loop before clip 9; after clip 9 completes, its final frame remains visible until sound has continuously returned to the existing quiet threshold, then clip 10 plays and returns to calm. Sustained renewed noise can interrupt clip 10. Passive and Individual retain the existing clip 7–8 recovery. Source animations have white backgrounds; composite their red dotted artwork onto the wall without an opaque rectangular panel.

Use real browser microphone input with explicit permission, smoothing and hysteresis. Any dB display is an uncalibrated estimate, not measured SPL. Passive / individual / group are manually selected scenarios, not automatic speaker counting.
